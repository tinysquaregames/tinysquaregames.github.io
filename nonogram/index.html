Tiny Square Nonogram

Logic puzzles, no guessing.

About

Fill the grid using the numbers on each row and column. Every one of the 1,000 puzzles (5×5 to 20×20, in three difficulty levels) has a single solution that you can reach by logic alone. Your progress is saved on your device and the game works offline. Available in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Support

Found a bug or have a suggestion? Email tinysquaregames@gmail.com with your device model and a short description. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I zoom on big grids? Use two fingers to pinch. The clues stay pinned to the edges of the screen.

How do I change the language? Tap the globe icon on the home screen.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is kept on your device. Deleting the app erases it.
