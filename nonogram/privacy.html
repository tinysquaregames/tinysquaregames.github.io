Privacy Policy

Tiny Square Nonogram · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use the mobile game Tiny Square Nonogram (the “App”) on iOS and Android, published by Tiny Square Games (“we”, “us”). If you have questions, contact us at tinysquaregames@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers for the App, so we do not receive or store your game data.

Your game progress (solved puzzles, puzzles in progress and your language choice) is stored only on your device. It is deleted if you delete the App.

2. Advertising

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • information about your device and the App, such as model, operating system and language;
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is handled by Google under its own terms. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can review your choice in the App settings.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission before accessing the IDFA (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using the IDFA. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads).

4. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising described above, we do not share data with third parties.

6. Security and retention

Data in transit to Google is encrypted. Progress stored on your device stays there until you delete the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google through the links above, but you can always contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App’s release notes.
